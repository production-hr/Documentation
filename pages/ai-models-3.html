3. Precision & Quantisation

How weights are stored and computed — from BF16 to Q4 GGUF — and which formats work best for image DiT models.

How weights are stored and computed. Image DiT models tolerate quantisation better than UNet models (no conv2d layers), but are more sensitive than LLMs. Prefer Q5_K_M or higher for image quality.

Format Bit-width Notes for Image Models Quality Typical VRAM Impact
BF16 16-bit brain float Standard for all modern DiT image models (Flux, Qwen-Image, Z-Image). Most numerically stable FP16 variant. Best quality Needs full VRAM: ~16 GB (12B), ~40 GB (20B)
FP16 16-bit float Older GPU standard (pre-Ampere). Functionally equivalent to BF16 for inference. Slightly less stable in training. Excellent Same VRAM as BF16
FP8 8-bit float (E4M3/E5M2) H100/A100 native format. Near-BF16 quality at half memory. Recommended for Qwen-Image-2512 and Flux.2 Dev on 24 GB GPUs. Very good ~20 GB for Qwen-Image-2512
Q8 / INT8 (GGUF) 8-bit integer Near-lossless for DiT models. Best GGUF option when VRAM allows. Qwen Q8 GGUF runs on 16 GB 4060 Ti. Near-lossless ~21 GB for Qwen-Image-2512
Q6_K (GGUF) ~6-bit Excellent quality. Negligible perceptual difference from Q8. Recommended if Q8 is too large. Near-lossless ~16 GB for 12B model
Q5_K_M (GGUF) ~5-bit Good balance for mid-range GPUs. Recommended floor for image models (more sensitive than LLMs). Good ~12 GB for 12B model
Q4_K_M (GGUF) ~4-bit Most popular local format. Noticeable softening in fine texture/detail on image models. Widely used for Flux. Acceptable ~8–9 GB for 12B model
NF4 4-bit normalfloat (bitsandbytes) Used in HuggingFace BNB quantisation. Marginally better than INT4 at same width. Acceptable Similar to Q4
NVF4 NVIDIA 4-bit float (Nunchaku) RTX 50-series (Blackwell) specific. TensorRT-optimised. Very fast on 5090/5080. Good (Blackwell) RTX 5090/5080 only
Q3 / Q2 (GGUF) ~3–2-bit Heavy quality loss on image models. Only use as absolute last resort on very limited hardware. Poor Minimum VRAM but visible artefacts
Rule of thumb: GGUF file size in GB ≈ VRAM required. Q8 Qwen-Image-2512 (21 GB) runs on a 16 GB 4060 Ti with some CPU offloading.
