6. Reading a Model Filename

Two annotated examples showing how to decode the naming conventions used by Flux, Qwen, and GGUF model files.

Example A: flux2-dev-Q4_K_M.gguf

Part Meaning
flux2-dev Model family + variant: Flux version 2, [dev] (open weights, non-commercial, ~32B)
Q4_K_M 4-bit quantisation, K-quant method, Medium variant
.gguf File format — loads via Unet Loader (GGUF) node, place in models/unet/ or models/diffusion_models/

Text encoder needed: Mistral-3 small → models/text_encoders/  |  VAE: flux2-vae.safetensors → models/vae/

Example B: qwen-image-2512-fp8.safetensors

Part Meaning
qwen-image Qwen-Image model family (Alibaba Tongyi)
2512 Date code: year 25, month 12 → December 2025 revision
fp8 8-bit float quantisation — ~20 GB, near-BF16 quality
.safetensors Safe format, memory-mapped. Place in models/diffusion_models/

Text encoder needed: Qwen-VL encoder → models/text_encoders/  |  VAE → models/vae/
