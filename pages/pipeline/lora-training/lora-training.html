This document tracks the ongoing development of the LoRA training pipeline for the video models in active use on the production. LoRAs are trained per model — a LoRA built for WAN 2.1 is not portable to LTX 2.3 or any WAN 2.2 variant. As the model roster evolves and base models are updated, the LoRA library must be maintained in parallel. This document records the training approach, dataset requirements, and current status for each model family.

Training Objectives

LoRAs are trained to address specific consistency and quality gaps that the base video models do not solve reliably at production scale. The training program covers six areas:

  • Character consistency — anchoring identity across shots, angles, and lighting conditions
  • Character detailing — recovering fine facial and body detail that generation or face swap passes lose
  • Hyper-realistic output — pushing skin texture, lighting response, and material definition beyond the base model default
  • Cloth simulation — maintaining fabric behaviour, wrinkle patterns, and garment silhouette across motion
  • Hair simulation — style, colour, and motion behaviour specific to each character
  • Props and accessories — gloves, hats, jewelry, and other identity-critical items that frequently drift or disappear under generation
