LTX 2.3 LoRA Training

Three LoRA-based workflows for LTX 2.3: Image-to-Video, Video-to-Video refinement, and performance-driven generation via ICLoRA.

  1. Image to Video — Audio Driven
  2. Video to Video
  3. Performance Driven Video — ICLoRA

For LTX 2.3, identity LoRAs were trained on synthetic data — a collection covering different expressions, camera angles, and generated performance stills across varied setups, wardrobe, and lighting conditions. Training was run on fal.ai at step counts of 1500, 1750, and 2000. The model supports three distinct workflows: audio-driven Image-to-Video generation, a Video-to-Video identity refinement pass, and performance-driven generation using ICLoRA as a ControlNet substitute.

Training location: Trained LoRA weights are stored at M:\HyperProductions\hypermodels\Ozzy\08_AI\_LORAS\LTX_2.3.

Image to Video — Audio Driven

The Image-to-Video pass uses an audio signal to drive expression and head motion. The LoRA engages from the first frame, anchoring character identity throughout the clip rather than drifting toward a base-model average over time.

Performance Driven Video — ICLoRA

LTX 2.3 has no native ControlNet support, but ICLoRA provides equivalent guidance. It accepts depth maps, Canny edges, OpenPose skeletons, or a combination of signals to drive performance while the identity LoRA holds character likeness. The result is controllable performance generation without a separate ControlNet model.
